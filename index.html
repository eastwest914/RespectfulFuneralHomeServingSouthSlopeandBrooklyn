Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start arranging a funeral?

Call our office and we will walk you through the first steps, including transfer of your loved one and a planning meeting.


Do you offer cremation without a funeral service?

Yes. We provide cremation services with or without a ceremony, and can arrange a memorial at a time that suits your family.


What is a graveside service?

A graveside service is a short ceremony held at the burial site. It is a simple, meaningful alternative to a full funeral.


Can I rent a casket?

Yes. Casket rental is available for families choosing cremation who still want a viewing or service. Ask us for details.

Funeral Services

Our funeral services are built around what your loved one valued. We plan visitation hours, the main ceremony, music, readings and transportation, and we make sure family members know where to be and when.

Whether your tradition calls for a large gathering or an intimate farewell, we adjust every detail. We also help with obituary notices, flowers, memorial cards and coordination with the clergy or officiant you choose.

Burial Services

Burial involves many moving parts, and we manage them so your family can focus on one another. We help you choose a casket, arrange the plot or cemetery appointment, file the required forms and schedule the funeral procession.

If you already own a burial space, we coordinate directly with the cemetery. If you are starting from scratch, we explain what to ask, what to compare and how to stay within the budget you have set.

Cremation Services

We offer cremation for families who want a simple, flexible way to honor a life. You may choose a traditional funeral with cremation afterward, a direct cremation or a memorial held on a date that suits everyone.

Our team walks you through authorizations, timing and container requirements, then helps you select an urn or keepsake. We can also advise on scattering ashes, so your decision feels thoughtful and complete.

Memorial Services

A memorial service gives family and friends a space to share stories, music and memories. It can be small and private or large and festive, and it can be held days, weeks or months after a passing.

We help you design the program, display photographs and mementos, set up seating and invite guests. Many families choose a memorial after cremation, and our staff will make sure the day feels warm and personal.

Funeral Pre-Planning

Pre-planning allows you to make your own funeral choices today, instead of leaving them to relatives in a moment of grief. We discuss burial or cremation, the kind of service you want and any religious or cultural requirements.

You can review costs in advance, put your wishes in writing and name who should carry them out. There is no obligation, and you can revisit your plan whenever life changes. Many people tell us it brings real peace of mind.

Casket Rental and Graveside Service

With casket rental, families can hold a viewing or full funeral service before a cremation without buying a casket for one use. It is a respectful way to keep tradition while managing cost.

We also arrange graveside services, which are brief ceremonies at the burial site with prayers, readings and personal words. They are simple to organize, easy for guests to attend and deeply meaningful for those who gather.
